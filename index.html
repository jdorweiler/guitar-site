About the workshop

One builder,
one bench.

I build one-off electric guitars from start to finish, with each instrument shaped by decisions about wood, tone, playability, and aesthetics.

The result is an instrument with continuity in every detail: the way the neck is carved, the way the frets are dressed, the way the finish is built up to keep the figure alive. Built to be played, not displayed.
