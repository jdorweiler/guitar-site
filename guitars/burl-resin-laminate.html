Burl Resin Laminate

Features a burl resin top with laminated walnut/wenge/maple back, Macassar ebony fretboard with brass and burl inlays, and Seymour Duncan JB/Jazz pickups. 24.75" scale with Schaller wraparound bridge.

Completed build

Body & construction

Body Burl resin top, walnut / wenge / maple back laminate
Neck Walnut, maple, wenge
Fretboard Macassar ebony with brass inlays and inlaid burl
Scale length 24.75"
Knobs Matching burl resin, made to complement the top

Electronics & hardware

Bridge pickup Seymour Duncan JB
Neck pickup Seymour Duncan Jazz
Bridge Schaller wraparound
Tuners Hipshot locking
